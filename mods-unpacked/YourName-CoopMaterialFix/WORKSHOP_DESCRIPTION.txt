Co-op Material Fix

Makes negative character material-drop modifiers PERSONAL in co-op.

Fixed:
• -50% materials dropped
• -50% materials dropped from enemies

Examples:
Streamer + normal character:
• Normal player keeps a normal material share
• Streamer's penalty applies only to Streamer

Fisherman + normal character:
• Enemy drops are no longer globally reduced for the teammate
• Fisherman's enemy-drop penalty applies only to Fisherman's own share
• Tree/non-enemy materials are not affected by Fisherman's enemy-only penalty

UI:
Affected lines now say:
• -50% materials dropped (only applies to you)
• -50% materials dropped from enemies (only applies to you)

Also handles:
• 2-4 player co-op
• merged material blobs
• uncollected bonus materials across waves
• Builder's uncollected-material conversion
• 1-value pickups without rounding them permanently to zero

Solo behavior is unchanged.

Target: Brotato 1.1.15.4

UI note: the '(only applies to you)' suffix is added only to negative material-drop effects on character descriptions, not to unrelated item effects.
