[h1]Co-op Material Fix[/h1]

Makes negative character material-drop modifiers [b]personal[/b] in co-op.

In vanilla co-op, one character's material penalty quietly shrinks the drops for the whole team. With this mod, only the player who picked that character pays for it.

[h2]Fixed effects[/h2]
[list]
[*]-50% materials dropped
[*]-50% materials dropped from enemies
[/list]

[h2]Examples[/h2]
[b]Streamer + normal character[/b]
[list]
[*]Normal player keeps a normal material share
[*]Streamer's penalty applies only to Streamer
[/list]

[b]Fisherman + normal character[/b]
[list]
[*]Enemy drops are no longer reduced for the teammate
[*]Fisherman's enemy-drop penalty applies only to Fisherman's own share
[*]Tree / non-enemy materials are not affected by Fisherman's enemy-only penalty
[/list]

[h2]UI[/h2]
Affected character lines now say:
[list]
[*]-50% materials dropped [i](only applies to you)[/i]
[*]-50% materials dropped from enemies [i](only applies to you)[/i]
[/list]
Only negative material-drop effects on characters get the label, not unrelated item effects.

[h2]Also handles[/h2]
[list]
[*]2-4 player co-op, multiple affected players at once
[*]Merged material blobs
[*]Uncollected bonus materials across waves
[*]Builder's uncollected-material conversion
[*]1-value pickups without rounding them down to zero
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Solo: unchanged
[*]ImprovedTooltips: supported, label shows in its tooltips too
[*]Other mods that change main.gd / run_data.gd: should work, load-order sensitive
[/list]

Target: Brotato 1.1.15.4, ModLoader 6.2
