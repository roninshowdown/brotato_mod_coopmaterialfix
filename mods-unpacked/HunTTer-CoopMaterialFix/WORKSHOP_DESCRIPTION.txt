💥 In vanilla Brotato co-op, a character's negative material modifier is [b]averaged across the whole party[/b] and then applied to every drop - for [b]everyone[/b].

In a 2-player run with Streamer, [b]both[/b] players lose 25% of their materials. Your teammate pays for [i]your[/i] character's downside.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/roninshowdown/brotato_mod_coopmaterialfix/main/assets/banner_fix.png[/img]

[code]
🪙 material drops
  ├─ 🧑 Player A (Streamer, -50%)  → ✅ penalty applies to A only
  └─ 🧑 Player B (Normal)          → ✅ full, unpenalized share
[/code]

[list]
[*]Enemy-only penalties never touch tree materials
[*]Positive modifiers (Jack, Evil Hat, Starfish) stay shared as vanilla intends
[*]Solo play is untouched
[/list]
